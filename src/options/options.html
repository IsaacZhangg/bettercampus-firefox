Settings backup

Firefox deletes an add-on's settings when it's removed, including at restart for one loaded from about:debugging. Installing a new build over the old one keeps them.

Automatic backup

BetterCampus keeps a copy of your settings in your Canvas site's storage, which stays when the add-on is removed. After a fresh install, opening Canvas puts the copy back.

Backup file

Save your settings to a file, or load them from one: for another computer or Firefox profile, or after clearing your Canvas site's data.
